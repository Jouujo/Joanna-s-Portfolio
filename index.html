About Me

Hi! I'm Joanna Chimalilo — a Computer Science and Mathematics student at Texas Tech University, graduating in May 2027. Originally from Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, my journey in technology is deeply driven by purpose: engineering technical systems that solve complex computational problems and creating pathways for communities that need them most.

That dedication to giving back birthed Kimbilio la Binti, an educational initiative supporting girls in Tanzania through mentorship, school outreach, and resource distribution. Whether building scientific software pipelines or community initiatives, my goal is to deliver lasting, tangible impact.

Technically, my focus spans software engineering, full-stack systems, and scientific computing. Currently, I work as an Applied Research Engineer at STAR Labs, participate in the Break Through Tech ('26–'27) fellowship, previously developed production pipelines as a Software Engineering Intern at Classed Inc, and conducted computational astrophysics research with the University of Maryland GRAD-MAP program.

On campus, I serve as a Master Peer Tutor at the TECHniques Center (where I built an internal AI session notes optimizer adopting the Gemini API) and represent our college as a Whitacre College of Engineering Ambassador.

I am actively seeking New Grad Software Engineering, Full-Stack Development, and Data Engineering roles starting Summer 2027.
